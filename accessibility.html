Accessibility

Everyone deserves a seat at the table — online too.

Hummus Republic is committed to making our website usable for as many people as possible. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and the site is built with keyboard-navigable controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, semantic structure and colour contrast checked against that standard.

What we have tested

Every page is checked with automated WCAG 2.1 A and AA testing, and currently passes with no violations. Automated testing catches roughly a third to a half of accessibility problems, so it is a floor rather than a guarantee — manual keyboard and screen-reader testing is ongoing, and we do not claim full conformance.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is never finished. We continue to test with assistive technologies and welcome feedback that helps us improve.

Need help or spot an issue?

If you have trouble accessing any part of this site, please let us know and we'll help and fix it.
